# My Integrations

#### NOTE
This feature is available in the OroCommerce Enterprise Edition.

#### HINT
This section is part of the [Integration Flows Concept Guide](../../../concept-guides/administration/integration-flows/index.md#concept-guide-integration-flows) topic that provides a general understanding of Activepieces integration flows, how they work with OroCommerce, and the business processes that they can automate.

**My Integrations** opens your personal workspace in <a href="https://www.activepieces.com/" target="_blank">Activepieces</a>, a third-party automation platform that is embedded in the OroCommerce back-office. In this workspace, you can build and manage your own integration flows. For example, you can create a flow that sends you a notification when a record is assigned to you.

The flows that you create in **My Integrations** run on your behalf and with your permissions. Flows that are shared by the whole team are available in [System Flows](../../system/integrations/activepieces-integration.md#user-guide-integrations-activepieces-system-flows).

#### NOTE
**My Integrations** is available when an administrator has enabled [Integration Flow Designer](../../system/configuration/system/integrations/integration-flow-designer.md#admin-configuration-integration-flow-designer-settings) and has granted your role the **My Integrations** permission.

To reach **My Integrations**:

1. Click on your user name at the top right of the application page.
2. Click **My Integrations**.

![The My Integrations page](user/img/system/integrations/activepieces/my-user-my-integrations.png)

The first time you open **My Integrations**, the application creates your Activepieces user. If an administrator has enabled the **Enable Connection Autocreation For Users** setting, the application also creates the connection to OroCommerce automatically. As a result, you can create flows immediately, without connecting Activepieces to OroCommerce manually.

From the **My Integrations** page, you can:

* [Create a flow](../../system/integrations/activepieces-integration.md#user-guide-integrations-activepieces-create-flow)
* [Review the executions of your flows and manage your connections](../../system/integrations/activepieces-integration.md#user-guide-integrations-activepieces-workspace)
